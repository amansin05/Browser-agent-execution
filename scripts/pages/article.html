Choosing the Right Pair of Jeans

By the Style Journal team

Denim is one of the most versatile fabrics in any wardrobe, but the sheer number of cuts, washes, and fits can make buying a new pair surprisingly difficult. This guide walks through the decisions that matter most so you can shop with confidence.

Fit comes first

Before worrying about colour or brand, settle on a fit. A slim straight leg flatters most builds, while a relaxed or tapered cut trades a little structure for all-day comfort. Try the same size across two fits before deciding — sizing varies more between cuts than between brands.

Wash and fabric

A darker rinse reads as more formal and hides wear, whereas a lighter, distressed wash leans casual. Look for denim with a small percentage of elastane if you want stretch, but pure cotton will age and fade more attractively over time.

Where the value is

Mid-range labels such as Levi's, Wrangler, and Lee consistently offer the best balance of durability and price. Premium brands add finish and detailing, but for an everyday pair the difference rarely justifies the cost. Buy the best fit you can find first, then optimise for price.
